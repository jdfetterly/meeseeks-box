## Objective

Make the work board the primary organizational surface and make launch flows fast, reliable, and traceable across iPhone and desktop workflows.

## Scope Summary

- In scope:
  - Kanban-first work organization
  - saved launch presets
  - one-shot scheduling from launch flow
  - chat escalation into work cards and runs
  - focused Playwright validation for golden paths
- Out of scope:
  - exhaustive UI automation
  - native iOS behaviors
