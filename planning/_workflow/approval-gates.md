# Approval Gates

## Gate 1: FDD Approval

Do not treat a feature as ready for full technical design until:
- scope is clear
- workflows are defined
- requirement IDs are present
- open questions are isolated

## Gate 2: TDD Approval

Do not treat a feature as ready for implementation planning until:
- architecture and contracts are defined
- major risks are surfaced
- test design is covered
- rollout concerns are captured

## Draft Mode

The current Meeseek Box feature artifacts are draft planning artifacts where noted. They are useful for shaping work, but they do not replace eventual formal FDD/TDD approval.
